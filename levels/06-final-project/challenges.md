# Challenges — Store sales report

Use the original database. Compare your output with solutions/results.md in the matching level folder after trying. Different queries can be equally correct.

## Challenge 1

Calculate total revenue in euros from paid orders. Name the column revenue_euros.

## Challenge 2

Show month and revenue_euros for paid orders in each month, ordered by month.

## Challenge 3

Show the three customers with the highest paid totals. Use name and total_euros; sort by total descending, then customer id to break ties.
