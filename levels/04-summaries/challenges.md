# Challenges — Counting and grouping

Use the original database. Compare your output with solutions/results.md in the matching level folder after trying. Different queries can be equally correct.

## Challenge 1

Count all customers. Name the column total_customers.

## Challenge 2

For each category, show category and average price in euros, rounded to two decimal places as average_euros. Sort by category.

## Challenge 3

Show city and total customers for cities with at least two customers. Name the count total and sort by city.
