# Challenges — Inserting, updating and deleting safely

Use the original database. Compare your output with solutions/results.md in the matching level folder after trying. Different queries can be equally correct.

## Challenge 1

In a transaction, insert customer 99, Test, Faro, with no email. Query that customer, then undo everything.

## Challenge 2

In a transaction, change product 2 to 2200 cents. Query its id, name and price_cents, then undo everything.

## Challenge 3

In a transaction, delete customer 6, who has no orders. Count the remaining customers as total_customers, then undo everything.
