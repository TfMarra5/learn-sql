# Challenges — Joining tables

Use the original database. Compare your output with solutions/results.md in the matching level folder after trying. Different queries can be equally correct.

## Challenge 1

Show order id, customer name and status, ordered by order id.

## Challenge 2

Show every customer and their order count, including zero. Use columns name and total_orders, ordered by customer id.

## Challenge 3

Show names of customers without orders, ordered by customer id.
